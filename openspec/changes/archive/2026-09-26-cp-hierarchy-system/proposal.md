## Why

Simnani Estate already has the skeleton of a Channel Partner (CP) network — Head CP, Company CP, Digital CP and Field CP each have an admin workspace (`/admin/freelancer-cp/*`) and a shared public dashboard (`/portal/freelancer`). But the actual chain of command doesn't match how the business wants to run it: there is no way for Head CP to hand a property/project down to a Company CP, no way for Company CP to delegate it onward to a Field or Digital CP, and no field/digital-specific workflow once a project lands with them. Worse, leads currently route straight to whichever CP type generated them (Digital and Field leads bypass Company CP entirely and only coincidentally also appear in the Head CP view via a filter) — there is no single "leads always land with Head CP first" inbox, and no explicit "forward to Company CP" action. Without this, Head CP has no real oversight of new business and CPs can't be assigned work in a controlled, top-down way.

## What Changes

- **Property/project assignment chain (top-down)**:
  - Properties list (`/admin/properties`) gets an **"Assign to Company CP"** row action and an assignment-status column. Head CP picks a Company CP from the CP network; this creates a `cpAssignment` record and does not touch the public property listing itself.
  - Company CP's workspace/dashboard gets an **"Assigned Projects"** view. For each project handed down by Head CP, Company CP chooses **"Delegate to Field CP"** or **"Delegate to Digital CP"**, picking a specific partner from their own network. This is a second-level assignment (`parentAssignmentId` links back to the Head CP → Company CP record).
  - Field CP and Digital CP each get an **"Assigned Projects"** view scoped to only what Company CP delegated to them.
- **Field CP workflow** (site-visit / on-ground conversion): schedule site visit → log visit outcome (attended/no-show, notes) → move through the existing lead pipeline (Site Visit Scheduled → Completed → Negotiation → Converted/Lost) → commission on conversion.
- **Digital CP workflow** (promotion / ad-led conversion): upload campaign creative/video for an assigned project → submit for moderation (reuses existing `VideoModerationDialog` approve/suggest-edit flow) → track ad-generated leads and campaign performance → commission on conversion.
- **Universal lead intake to Head CP (BREAKING routing change)**: every new lead — whether generated by a Digital CP's ad/link or logged manually by a Field CP from ground activity — is created with `routingStage: "head-cp"` and shows up first in the Head CP inbox, regardless of `submittedBy.cpType`. This replaces the current incidental filter (`leadCpTypes: ["digital","field"]`) where Digital/Field leads were already visible to Head CP by coincidence and never routed through Company CP.
- **Forward-to-Company-CP action**: Head CP inbox gets a **"Forward to Company CP"** action per lead, setting `routingStage: "company-cp"` and `assignedTo`. Company CP's Leads tab is re-scoped to only leads at `routingStage: "company-cp"` (today it only shows leads it submitted itself). Company CP keeps (and extends) the existing "Assign to…" delegation select to also support Digital CP, not just Field CP.
- Admin and public portal both change: the admin CP workspaces (`CPTypeWorkspace.jsx` and its four pages) get the new routing/assignment logic; `/portal/freelancer` (the public-facing dashboard shared by Company/Digital/Field accounts) mirrors the same "Assigned Projects" and lead-visibility rules so a logged-in CP sees the same state as their admin-managed record.

**BREAKING**: Lead visibility rules change — Digital CP and Field CP no longer see their own submitted leads directly; leads must pass through Head CP → Company CP → (optionally) back down before a Field/Digital CP sees them again. Existing demo data in `lib/demoPortal.js` (`CP_LEADS`) and `lib/adminStorage.js` seed leads will need a `routingStage` field or they will not appear anywhere.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `cp-property-assignment`: Top-down property/project delegation — Head CP → Company CP → Field CP/Digital CP, including assignment records, UI actions, and per-role "Assigned Projects" views.
- `cp-lead-routing`: Universal lead intake to a Head CP inbox regardless of source, plus explicit forward/delegate actions (Head CP → Company CP → Field/Digital CP) replacing the current source-based visibility filter.
- `field-cp-workflow`: Site-visit scheduling, visit logging, and conversion pipeline actions available to a Field CP once a project/lead is delegated to them.
- `digital-cp-workflow`: Campaign/creative upload, moderation, and ad-performance tracking available to a Digital CP once a project is delegated to them.

### Modified Capabilities
(none — no existing spec files under `openspec/specs/`; the current CP admin/portal code has no formal spec, so all behavior above is captured as new capabilities)

## Impact

- `app/(admin)/admin/properties/page.js` — add "Assign to Company CP" row action + assignment-status column.
- `components/admin/freelancer-cp/CPTypeWorkspace.jsx` — rework lead scoping from `submittedBy.cpType`-based filtering to `routingStage`-based filtering; add Forward/Delegate actions; add "Assigned Projects" tab.
- `app/(admin)/admin/freelancer-cp/{head-cp,company,digital,field}/page.js` — updated `leadCpTypes`/props to match new routing model; digital/field pages get workflow-specific tabs (site visits / campaign creatives).
- New dialogs: `components/admin/freelancer-cp/AssignPropertyDialog.jsx` (Head CP → Company CP, Company CP → Field/Digital CP), `components/admin/freelancer-cp/SiteVisitLogDialog.jsx` (Field CP visit logging).
- `lib/adminStorage.js` — new `ADMIN_KEYS.cpAssignments` collection + helper functions (`addCpAssignment`, `forwardLead`, `delegateLead`); existing `cpLeads` records gain `routingStage`.
- `app/portal/freelancer/page.js`, `components/portal/freelancer/FreelancerPortalClient.jsx`, `components/portal/channel-partner/LeadCard.jsx` — mirror "Assigned Projects" tab and new lead-visibility rules on the public dashboard.
- `lib/demoPortal.js` — extend `CP_LEADS` with `routingStage`; add demo `CP_PROJECT_ASSIGNMENTS` data.
- `app/api/leads/route.js`, `app/api/leads/[id]/route.js` — extend lead payload with `routingStage`/`assignedTo` so the real-API path (with localStorage fallback, per existing pattern) supports the new forward/delegate actions.
- No changes to auth: Head CP continues to mean "logged into `/admin`" — no new admin role/permission system is introduced, since only one Head CP exists per the stated plan.
